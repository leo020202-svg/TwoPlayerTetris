About Two Player Tetris

Two Player Tetris is a small, independent browser game made by a solo developer. It started with a simple question: what if two people played the classic falling-blocks puzzle together instead of against each other?

Why co-op?

Most multiplayer block puzzles are head-to-head races. They're great if you're evenly matched, but less fun when one of you is much faster. Here you share one board and one score, so a beginner and an expert can play together and both matter. Six modes change how you cooperate — sharing the space, splitting the board, taking turns, or even splitting the controls of a single piece.

How it works

  • No accounts. You get a room code the moment the page loads; share it and play.
  • No install. It runs in any modern browser on desktop, laptop, phone or tablet.
  • No ads, no tracking. There are no advertising or analytics scripts on the site. See the privacy page for exactly what data the game handles.
  • Real-time. A small game server keeps both players' boards in sync over a WebSocket connection, and your own moves are shown instantly in your browser so the controls stay responsive.
